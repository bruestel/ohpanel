The second test build of 0.10.0: everything of
[0.10.0-rc1](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/releases/tag/v0.10.0-rc1) (the NSPanel US, still not tried on a real
US panel), and covers whose up / stop / down go to an item of their own.

**New**
- A cover page can send up / stop / down to an item of its own, for covers that take their position on one item (a
  Dimmer) and open / stop / close on another (a String item taking `open`, `stop`, `close`): `"commands": {"item":
  "Curtain_Command", "open": "open", "stop": "stop", "close": "close"}`, in the web interface the field "Command
  item". Dragging on the drawing and the presets still send the position to the page's item; the words go as they
  read, also on a reversed cover.
- On a cover group page each member names its own command item in its metadata (`cover=" "
  [command_item="Curtain_Command"]`); up / stop / down on the whole group then go to each member on its own.
  ([docs/panel-config.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.10.0-rc2/docs/panel-config.md#cover))

**Fixed**
- A member opened from a cover group page as a page of its own sends the group page's command words (`commands`),
  not UP / STOP / DOWN.

**Install**
- Already running OHpanel on an NSPanel EU: Settings > Firmware with `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.10.0-rc2.bin`
  from `ohpanel-0.10.0-rc2.zip`. The display design is unchanged (`ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-4508cd3f.tft`); coming
  from 0.9.2 or older, write it once.
- An NSPanel US: flash `ohpanel-nspanel-us-factory-0.10.0-rc2.bin` over the serial port, flash erased first
  ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/main/docs/install.md); the browser flasher has only
  the latest release, without the US firmware yet); then write `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-fc4305ba.tft`. Already
  on rc1: only the firmware, `ohpanel-nspanel-us-firmware-0.10.0-rc2.bin`.
- Coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image over the serial port, flash erased first. An
  update from Tasmota over WiFi keeps Tasmota's partition table, in which OHpanel cannot store its configuration.
- `ohpanel-0.10.0-rc2.zip` holds, for each panel, the factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates
  over WiFi and the display design, and the licences that go with the binaries.
