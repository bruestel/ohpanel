The first release of OHpanel: ESP-IDF firmware for the Sonoff NSPanel EU that talks to openHAB directly, over its
event stream and REST API. No MQTT broker, no rules to keep the panel in sync.

**What it does**
- Pages of your own: buttons, lists of values, lights (brightness, colour temperature, colour wheel), covers and awnings
  (alone or as a group, with presets), thermostats, a media player with its cover and a station list, the weather
  forecast, QR codes
- A home screen with the weather, temperatures, status icons, shortcuts, two info lines and the two relays
- Set up in its own web interface with a live preview of the display; 25 display languages
- Setup without a cable after the first flash, updates of the firmware and the display design over WiFi
- HTTPS with a certificate authority of the panel's own, or one shared by all your panels; backups
- WiFi: the strongest access point of a network, roaming with 802.11k/v where the network leads it

**Install**
- First installation over the serial port: [docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/main/docs/install.md),
  or in the browser: https://bruestel.github.io/ohpanel/flash/
- `ohpanel-0.9.0.zip` holds the factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates over WiFi, the display
  design `bc629e1e` and the licences that go with the binaries.
