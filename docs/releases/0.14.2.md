A longer log in the web interface: days instead of an hour.
The display design is the one of 0.13.0: panels on 0.13 or 0.14 need only the firmware.

**Changed**
- **The log holds 256 KB instead of 32 KB** (in PSRAM, which has plenty to spare). Diagnostics shows all of it when
  it opens, and *Download* saves all of it: handy for a log asked for in an issue.
- **Fewer status lines**: the network and openHAB lines were logged every minute along with the memory line, and
  filled the log in about an hour. The network and openHAB lines now come when they change, all three every
  15 minutes.
- `GET /api/v1/log` gives at most 16 KB a request and says `"more": true` while there is more to fetch from `next`;
  `since=0` starts at the oldest line it holds.

**Install**
- Panels on 0.13 or 0.14: Settings > Firmware > Check for updates installs the firmware; the display design stays.
- Panels on 0.12 or older: the same, then the display design it offers.
- An NSPanel EU by hand: `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.14.2.bin` from `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-0.14.2.zip` (display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-4e7603bf.tft`, unchanged).
- An NSPanel US by hand: the same from `ohpanel-nspanel-us-0.14.2.zip` (upright, display design
  `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-67c9b1ac.tft`) or `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-0.14.2.zip` (on its side, display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-display-26f73ca8.tft`).
- A new panel, or coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image of its zip over the serial port,
  flash erased first ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.14.2/docs/install.md)).
- Each zip holds that panel's factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates over WiFi and the display
  design, and the licences that go with the binaries.
