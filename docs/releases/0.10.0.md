OHpanel for the **NSPanel US** next to the EU, a cover's up, stop and down to an item of their own, and fixes:
everything of the test builds 0.10.0-rc1 to rc3. The US version has still not been tried on a real US panel; if you
have one, please tell us how it works (in an issue).

**New**
- The NSPanel US (the tall one, its screen upright) has a firmware and a display design of its own, laid out upright
  (320 x 480): on home the weather and the clock on top, the values, shortcuts and info lines above the relays, whose
  labels sit over the two buttons; button pages with 2 x 4 wide tiles. One configuration works on both panels.
- Each panel takes only its own firmware. To try one variant's design on the other panel, the API takes it with
  `?any_variant=1` ([docs/flashing.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.10.0/docs/flashing.md#updates)).
- The browser flasher asks which panel it is.
- The status page names the panel: NSPanel EU or NSPanel US.
- A cover page can send up, stop and down to an item of its own, for covers that take their position on one item and
  open, stop and close on another: `"commands": {"item": "Curtain_Command", ...}`, in the web interface "Command
  item"; on a cover group page per member in its metadata (`command_item`)
  ([docs/panel-config.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.10.0/docs/panel-config.md#cover)).

**Changed**
- The display design's version is worked out from the design itself, not from the files it is made from. With this
  release the EU's version changes once all the same (`bc629e1e` to `4508cd3f`), the design unchanged.

**Fixed**
- Changing brightness, idle brightness or the idle time on the panel's settings page restarted the panel a few
  seconds later, when the value was stored.
- A member opened from a cover group page as a page of its own sends the group page's command words.
- On the NSPanel US the web interface's home preview showed the relay buttons' places in the middle of the screen.

**Install**
- From 0.9.x on an NSPanel EU: Settings > Firmware with `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.10.0.bin` from
  `ohpanel-0.10.0.zip`, then write the display design `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-4508cd3f.tft` once (the same
  design, a new version). From 0.10.0-rc1, rc2 or rc3: only the firmware.
- An NSPanel US: flash it in the [browser](https://bruestel.github.io/ohpanel/flash/) or
  `ohpanel-nspanel-us-factory-0.10.0.bin` over the serial port, flash erased first
  ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.10.0/docs/install.md)); then write
  `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-fc4305ba.tft`. From a test build: only the firmware.
- Coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image over the serial port, flash erased first. An
  update from Tasmota over WiFi keeps Tasmota's partition table, in which OHpanel cannot store its configuration.
- `ohpanel-0.10.0.zip` holds, for each panel, the factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates over
  WiFi and the display design, and the licences that go with the binaries.
