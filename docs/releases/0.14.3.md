Commands over https resume their TLS session again with openHAB behind a public certificate, and an album cover no
longer takes most of the panel's internal memory while it is made.
The display design is the one of 0.13.0: panels on 0.13 or 0.14 need only the firmware.

**Fixed**
- **Resumed TLS sessions with a public certificate**: openHAB (Java) puts the server's certificate chain into its
  session tickets; with a public certificate such as Let's Encrypt's or Google's a ticket is about 4 KB. The panel
  sends the ticket back when it resumes the session, and its outgoing TLS buffer of 4 KB was too small for it: every
  resumed handshake failed with `mbedtls_ssl_handshake returned -0x008A`. Since 0.14.1 the command was sent once more
  with a full handshake, a second or two late; now the buffer is 16 KB, the most TLS allows, and the session resumes.
  The two warnings of 0.14.1 (`no connection on the saved TLS session`) should no longer come.
- **Album covers and internal memory**: turning a cover into rectangles kept a map of small entries, and these stay
  in the ESP32's internal RAM, the scarce kind: an 80 px cover took about 80 KB of it, and the least free internal
  RAM fell from about 93 KB to about 31 KB each time one was made. A TLS connection opened in that moment could fail
  for want of memory. Covers (and the QR code) are made without it now; the result is the same, rectangle for
  rectangle.
- The log line of a cover names its size and where it came from.

**Install**
- Panels on 0.13 or 0.14: Settings > Firmware > Check for updates installs the firmware; the display design stays.
- Panels on 0.12 or older: the same, then the display design it offers.
- An NSPanel EU by hand: `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.14.3.bin` from `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-0.14.3.zip` (display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-4e7603bf.tft`, unchanged).
- An NSPanel US by hand: the same from `ohpanel-nspanel-us-0.14.3.zip` (upright, display design
  `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-67c9b1ac.tft`) or `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-0.14.3.zip` (on its side, display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-display-26f73ca8.tft`).
- A new panel, or coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image of its zip over the serial port,
  flash erased first ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.14.3/docs/install.md)).
- Each zip holds that panel's factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates over WiFi and the display
  design, and the licences that go with the binaries.
