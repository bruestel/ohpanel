A new home screen: twelve shortcuts in three rows of four, and room for a departure time with AM/PM; notifications
that wake the screen only from a level, and then keep it on. A new display design: every panel needs the firmware
and the design.

**New**
- **Twelve shortcuts** on home (seven before), three rows of four, right-aligned. On the NSPanel EU and the NSPanel
  US on its side the clock and the date moved up a little to make room; on the NSPanel US upright the weather block.
- **The info lines' right field** (a departure time, mostly) holds 8 characters, so `12:41 PM` shows whole (5
  before: `12:41`); the main text is a little narrower for it (130 px on the NSPanel EU and on its side, 182 upright).
- **Which notifications wake the screen** (`display.notifications.wake_and_keep_on`, in the web interface
  under Notifications): `info`, `warning` or `alert`, that level and higher, or `never` (the default). One that
  wakes the screen keeps it on until its card is closed; one below wakes nothing, and its card shows once a touch
  wakes the screen.
- **"On when" with a limit**: a status icon or shortcut can count as on while its item's number is beyond one,
  `"on": {"above": 7}` (`below`, `at_least`, `at_most`), in the item's unit: an icon only while more than 7 W
  flow, say. In the web interface: On when, "a number beyond a limit".

**Changed**
- **A notification no longer wakes the screen** unless `wake_and_keep_on` says so: before, every one woke it for
  as long as a touch would. To have that back for alerts, say, set `"wake_and_keep_on": "alert"`.
- **The shortcuts are numbered as read**: `shortcuts[0..3]` the top row, `[4..7]` the middle one, `[8..11]` the
  lowest. Shortcuts configured before move: seven fill the top row and three places of the middle one, and the
  lowest row stays empty. To have them where they were, drag them in the web interface's preview of home (a
  shortcut dragged onto another place swaps with what is there) or move them in the configuration.
- **The NSPanel US upright**: the value rows' number ends before the shortcuts' left column. A number with its unit
  up to 4 digits (`1013 hPa`, `-12,5 °C`) shows whole, a longer one is shortened.
- **The web interface** shows "On when" of a status icon or shortcut only where it does something: shown while
  its item is on, or a shortcut that opens its page only then. Shown always, it changed nothing; the rules set the
  icon's look (for a number, `at most 0` rather than `equals OFF`).
- **The web interface's display settings** a little more even: every field labelled, as far apart.

**Fixed**
- **The preview** drew texts beside the info lines' badge (and other texts placed by their capitals) a pixel or two
  too small, so they looked top-aligned where the panel centres them.

**Install**
- Every panel: Settings > Firmware > Check for updates installs the firmware, then the display design it offers.
- An NSPanel EU by hand: `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.17.0.bin` from `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-0.17.0.zip`, then the
  display design `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-d78edeec.tft`.
- An NSPanel US by hand: the same from `ohpanel-nspanel-us-0.17.0.zip` (upright, display design
  `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-926cd92d.tft`) or `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-0.17.0.zip` (on its side, display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-display-61bfd2ee.tft`).
- A new panel, or coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image of its zip over the serial port,
  flash erased first ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.17.0/docs/install.md)).
- Each zip holds that panel's factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates over WiFi and the display
  design, and the licences that go with the binaries. `elf-ohpanel-0.17.0.zip`: the `.elf` files to decode a core
  dump ([docs/crash-analysis.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.17.0/docs/crash-analysis.md)).
