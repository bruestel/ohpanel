Melodies on the buzzer: in RTTTL, the ringtones' notation, from openHAB or as melodies of your own for touches and
notifications. Only the firmware: the display design is the one of 0.21.0.

**New**
- **Melodies from openHAB**: the play item (`display.sound.play`) takes a melody in RTTTL as well as a tone's name,
  e.g. `ode_to_joy:d=4,o=6,b=120:e,e,f,g,g,f,e,d,c,c,d,e,e.,8d,2d`, with `|30` for a volume as a tone. At most 400
  notes; what is no melody stays on the item.
- **Melodies of your own** (`display.sound.melodies`): up to 8 under names of your own, each the tone of a touch or
  of a notification's level, and played by its name on the play item (`doorbell|30`). A configuration with a melody
  that does not play is not saved, and says which note is wrong.
- **In the web interface**: the Sound tile lists your melodies, each with how many notes and how long, where it is
  used and its notes as a strip, and ▶ to play it on the panel before it is saved, ■ to stop it. *＋ Add melody*
  opens a new one to edit; what is typed is checked at once, a wrong note marked in the text. A melody removed can
  be brought back (*Undo*), with the tones that named it. Examples to start from, each to hear first, all public
  domain: Korobeiniki, *Für Elise*, *Ode to Joy*, *In the Hall of the Mountain King*, *The Entertainer* and the
  Westminster chimes.
- **`POST /api/v1/sound`** plays a melody too, and says why one does not play; `"tone": "none"` stops what plays.

**Fixed**
- **The display preview** drew the thermostat's flame (or snowflake) and the keypad's state icon too large and too
  high beside their words; it now draws them as the panel does.
