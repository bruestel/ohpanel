The third test build of 0.10.0: everything of
[0.10.0-rc2](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/releases/tag/v0.10.0-rc2) (the NSPanel US, still not tried on a real
US panel), and two fixes.

**Fixed**
- Changing brightness, idle brightness or the idle time on the panel's settings page restarted the panel a few
  seconds later, when the value was stored: storing it took more stack than the display's task had to spare. It is
  now stored in a task of its own (and no longer holds the page while the flash is written).
- On the NSPanel US the home preview in the web interface showed the places of the two relay buttons in the middle of
  the screen, over the shortcuts, where the NSPanel EU has them. They are now at the bottom, over the buttons.

**Install**
- Already running OHpanel on an NSPanel EU: Settings > Firmware with `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.10.0-rc3.bin`
  from `ohpanel-0.10.0-rc3.zip`. The display design is unchanged (`ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-4508cd3f.tft`); coming
  from 0.9.2 or older, write it once.
- An NSPanel US: flash `ohpanel-nspanel-us-factory-0.10.0-rc3.bin` over the serial port, flash erased first
  ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/main/docs/install.md); the browser flasher has only
  the latest release, without the US firmware yet); then write `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-fc4305ba.tft`. Already
  on rc1 or rc2: only the firmware, `ohpanel-nspanel-us-firmware-0.10.0-rc3.bin`.
- Coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image over the serial port, flash erased first. An
  update from Tasmota over WiFi keeps Tasmota's partition table, in which OHpanel cannot store its configuration.
- `ohpanel-0.10.0-rc3.zip` holds, for each panel, the factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates
  over WiFi and the display design, and the licences that go with the binaries.
