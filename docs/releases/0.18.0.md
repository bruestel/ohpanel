A button page can now take its tiles from an openHAB group: see which lights are on and switch them off. The display
design is the one of 0.17.0: panels on 0.17.0 or later need only the firmware.

**New**
- **A group's members as tiles**: a button page takes `"group"` instead of `"buttons"`. Each Switch, Dimmer or Color
  in it is a tile that toggles it (other members are left out), eight on a sheet; swipe for more, in the swipe row
  too, where its sheets come before the next page. `metadata` and `label` name, sort and hide the members as on an
  entity page.
- **An icon per member**: with `metadata`, a member's `icon` is its own instead of the page's, on a group's button
  page and on an entity page: `light="Hall" [icon="ceiling-light"]`.
- **Only what is on** (`"only_on": true`): the page lists the members that are on as it opens. A tile switched off
  stays, grey, until the page opens again, so the tiles do not move under a finger.
- **In the web interface**: Edit in the preview of a button page, Tiles: the members of a group.

```json
{"id": "lights-on", "type": "buttons", "title": "Lights on", "group": "gLights",
 "icon": "lightbulb", "only_on": true}
```

**Fixed**
- **The swipe row's dots** follow a group's members when they change while its page shows (an entity page's too).

**Install**
- Panels on 0.17.0 or later: Settings > Firmware > Check for updates installs the firmware; the display design stays.
- Panels on 0.16 or older: the same, then the display design it offers.
- An NSPanel EU by hand: `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.18.0.bin` from `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-0.18.0.zip` (display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-d78edeec.tft`, unchanged).
- An NSPanel US by hand: the same from `ohpanel-nspanel-us-0.18.0.zip` (upright, display design
  `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-926cd92d.tft`) or `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-0.18.0.zip` (on its side, display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-display-61bfd2ee.tft`).
- A new panel, or coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image of its zip over the serial port,
  flash erased first ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.18.0/docs/install.md)).
- Each zip holds that panel's factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates over WiFi and the display
  design, and the licences that go with the binaries. `elf-ohpanel-0.18.0.zip`: the `.elf` files to decode a core
  dump ([docs/crash-analysis.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.18.0/docs/crash-analysis.md)).
