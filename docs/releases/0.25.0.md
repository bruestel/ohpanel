A light background for eyes that find light text on dark hard to read, and a new display design for it: after the
firmware update (Settings > Firmware > Check for updates) write the display design it offers.

**New**
- **White** (`"background": "white"`): black on white, the dimmed parts black too, no grey on grey. Every tile the
  same light grey with black text; a tile that is **on** shows a ring in the accent colour (any accent, also one of
  your own) -- nothing in reversed colours to read. A button that only opens a page has no ring; the pills stay
  filled in the accent. The weather icons in their line style with black lines, the boot logo's words dark; the
  palette's yellow shows as orange, white as black, light grey as dark grey (badges too), a badge's text stays white.
  See *The White background* in the configuration docs.
- **Writing the display design tries again**: where it breaks off at the fast rate (the display stops confirming,
  the transfer is cut), the web interface writes it once more at the slower, proven rate -- about 30 minutes. For the
  update and for a file of your own.

**Changed**
- **Candy is gone** to make room for White: the display holds five backgrounds. A panel set to Candy shows Aurora;
  choose another background in the web interface.
- A button's name, state and bar sit 4 px higher on its tile (clear of White's ring).
- **Web interface**: the *Display* card stands above *Pages*, across the page; the background list has the default
  once ("Aurora (default)"), not twice.
- **The web preview** shows the weather icons as the display has them for each background, White's line style too.
  Its layout takes less of the firmware's flash (weather icons in one picture, backgrounds as WebP): the firmware
  has more room left than 0.24.

**Fixed**
- The cover group page's buttons: their arrows and the stop square sat a few pixels low. Now centred.
- The preview showed the coloured badges of the home screen's info lines without their number ("42" on red) since
  0.24.0. The display was not affected.
