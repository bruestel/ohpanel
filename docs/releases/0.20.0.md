A button and its relay can now be independent of each other: a relay only openHAB switches, for a load that must not
be switched at the wall. The display design is the one of 0.17.0: panels on 0.17.0 or later need only the firmware.

**New**
- **A relay with an item of its own** (`"relay": {"item": "..."}`): only that item switches the relay, no press does.
  The button acts on `item`, independently of the relay; without one it does nothing and the bar shows the relay.
  With no local switch left, such a relay stays as it is while openHAB or the network is away, on as well.
- **A relay that does not show** (`"hidden": true`): neither label nor bar on home, whatever the button and the relay
  do. Without it everything stays as it was.
- **In the web interface**: Edit in the preview of home, a button: one choice of what the button does, with the items
  it needs under it.

```json
"relays": [
  {"label": "Scene", "item": "Evening_Scene", "relay": {"item": "Pump"}},
  {"relay": {"item": "Heating_Contact"}, "hidden": true}
]
```

**Fixed**
- A relay given an item takes its state as the configuration is saved, not only when the item next changes.
- The web interface no longer warns that a relay's label is too wide on the NSPanel US on its side: it measured the
  upright label by its width.

**Install**
- Panels on 0.17.0 or later: Settings > Firmware > Check for updates installs the firmware; the display design stays.
- Panels on 0.16 or older: the same, then the display design it offers.
- An NSPanel EU by hand: `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.20.0.bin` from `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-0.20.0.zip` (display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-d78edeec.tft`, unchanged).
- An NSPanel US by hand: the same from `ohpanel-nspanel-us-0.20.0.zip` (upright, display design
  `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-926cd92d.tft`) or `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-0.20.0.zip` (on its side, display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-display-61bfd2ee.tft`).
- A new panel, or coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image of its zip over the serial port,
  flash erased first ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.20.0/docs/install.md)).
- Each zip holds that panel's factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates over WiFi and the display
  design, and the licences that go with the binaries. `elf-ohpanel-0.20.0.zip`: the `.elf` files to decode a core
  dump ([docs/crash-analysis.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.20.0/docs/crash-analysis.md)).
