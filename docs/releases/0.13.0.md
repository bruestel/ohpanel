OHpanel 0.13.0: animated icons of its own, the NSPanel US on its side, switching without the wait after a pause.
It needs the new display design on every panel.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bruestel/ohpanel/v0.13.0/docs/images/releases/0.13.0-animated-icons.gif" width="450" alt="Home with animated icons">

**New**
- **19 animated icons** of OHpanel's own, for shortcuts and status icons: dishwasher, oven, kitchen machine,
  kettle, coffee, fan, heat pump, radiator, fire, solar, grid feed-in and supply, battery charging, robot vacuum,
  garage, bell, siren and two sprinklers. Each is `ohpanel-<name>-1` to `-8`, shown in turn with an `interval`
  ([docs/panel-config.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.13.0/docs/panel-config.md)).
- **NSPanel US landscape**: a third variant, the NSPanel US mounted on its side with its buttons on the right. The
  display is used landscape over its whole 480 px; on home the relays' names stand upright beside their buttons,
  read from the bottom up. A US panel takes either US firmware, so it can be turned on the wall: install the other
  variant's firmware, then its display design
  ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.13.0/docs/install.md)).
- **The NSPanel US is tested** on a real panel and no longer marked untested. The install guide shows its board
  and wiring with photos, and how to save Sonoff's firmware before flashing.

**Changed**
- **Switching after a pause is quick**: the connection to openHAB keeps its TLS session while idle and resumes it,
  instead of a full handshake of a few seconds for the first command after a while. The session is renewed every
  hour while nothing waits on it.
- **The thermostat in the swipe row**: a finger that goes on sideways off the ring swipes to the next page and
  keeps the target; dragging along the ring still sets it.
- **Home's value rows**: their icons are larger (32 px) and centred on the numbers, the rows a little further apart.
  On the NSPanel US the clock, the date and the weather are centred.
- **Drawing**: a button tile whose state changes is redrawn in one go, without fields in the old colour for a
  moment; pills and rounded buttons (keypad, thermostat, modes, the cover page's up, stop and down) are drawn row by
  row from the top, no bars or squares showing first.

**Fixed**
- The web interface's update card: after an update it showed "Done" for good; it now looks again once the panel
  runs what was installed, and after a firmware offers the display design it needs.
- The mute button's word sat above its icon instead of beside it.

**Install**
- Panels on 0.12: Settings > Firmware > Check for updates installs the firmware, then the display design.
- An NSPanel EU by hand: `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.13.0.bin` from `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-0.13.0.zip`, then the
  display design `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-4e7603bf.tft`.
- An NSPanel US by hand: the same from `ohpanel-nspanel-us-0.13.0.zip` (upright, display design
  `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-67c9b1ac.tft`) or `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-0.13.0.zip` (on its side, display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-display-26f73ca8.tft`).
- A new panel, or coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image of its zip over the serial port,
  flash erased first ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.13.0/docs/install.md)).
- Each zip holds that panel's factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates over WiFi and the display
  design, and the licences that go with the binaries.
