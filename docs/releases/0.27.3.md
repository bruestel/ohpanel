A contact shows a window, as in openHAB, and camera pictures are safer on the memory. Only the firmware: the display
design is the one of 0.27.0.

**Changed**
- **openHAB's `contact` icon is a window** (thanks obones, forum #169): an item with the category `contact` showed a
  door; openHAB's classic `contact` icon is a window, open or closed, and the panel now shows that too. For a door
  sensor, give the item the category `door` (or `frontdoor`, `garagedoor`).

**Fixed**
- **Camera pictures and the memory**: a camera's snapshot could still restart the panel while its rectangles were
  put in order, or while the picture was scaled to the page. Both now take no more memory than is left; a picture
  that does not fit is not shown (the log says so) and the panel keeps running.
