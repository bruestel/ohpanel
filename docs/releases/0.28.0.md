Two covers side by side, the start page from openHAB, the front buttons pressed twice, three times or held, and a
relay button's label from an item. With a new display design: update the firmware first, then the display design
(Settings > Firmware > Check for updates).

**New**
- **Two covers on one page** (forum #181): a cover page with `"pair": [{"item": ..., "label": ...}, {...}]` shows
  both covers side by side, each drawn large with its position over it; a finger on one sets that one. Three pills
  at the top choose what up, stop and down move: the left one, both (as the page opens) or the right one. Each
  cover's style, moving item and command item come from its metadata. In the web interface: *Shows*, *two covers
  side by side*. Needs the new display design; an older one shows the left cover alone.
- **The start page from an openHAB item** (`display.start_page`, forum #183): while a String item names a page of
  the swipe row, that page is the start page. The panel goes back to it after `home_after`, wakes on it, and it
  has no close cross. A rule, or `expire` on the item, sets it for a while: the fermentation page while brewing,
  the heating while it is being set. Empty, NULL or a page not in the row: the row's first, as before. In the web
  interface: *Pages and openHAB*, *The start page*.
- **The front buttons pressed twice, three times or held** (forum #179): `"double"`, `"triple"` and `"hold"` on a
  relay each do one of what a press does (switch the relay, toggle an item, open a page). `"events"` sends a String
  item every press as Tasmota names them (`SINGLE`, `DOUBLE`, `TRIPLE`, `HOLD`, `CLEAR`) for rules. A single press
  waits a third of a second only where more is wanted; a hold counts after 0.8 s. In the web interface: the relay's
  *Advanced*.
- **A relay button's label from an item** (`"text": {"item", "format"?, "factor"?}`): the item's state as the
  label, so a rule can write it; while it is NULL, UNDEF or empty, the configured label shows. In the web
  interface: the relay's *Advanced*, which is folded and remembered open or closed.

**Changed**
- **Home's high and low as on the weather page** (forum #182): the high first, `↑22° ↓9°`. The display designs
  before keep working.
- **After flashing, run the panel mounted, on mains** (GitHub #11): the serial adapter's 3.3 V are enough for
  flashing, not once the WiFi starts; a panel fed by it may restart again and again. The install guide and the
  browser flasher's last steps say so.

**Fixed**
- **Frayed labels on pills**: the presets of a cover, the thermostat's modes and its on/off pill, the keypad's
  buttons. Their text was drawn without a background, so the font's smoothed edges were mixed with black. Now
  they are drawn on the pill's colour.
- **Text of the page before on the next one**: a state arriving while a page change was on its way drew on the
  new page (home's upright relay labels over the weather page, for example).
