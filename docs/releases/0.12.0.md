OHpanel 0.12.0: updates from the web interface, pages added from their type, a switch on the thermostat and on the
keypad, round discs. It needs the new display design on every panel.

**New**
- **Updates from the web interface**: Settings > Firmware > Check for updates finds the latest release (with "Test
  builds too" also a newer test build), shows what is new and installs the firmware, then the display design it
  needs. Size and SHA-256 are checked before anything is written
  ([docs/flashing.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.12.0/docs/flashing.md)). The browser
  flasher offers the test build too.
- **Adding a page**: "+ Add to the swipe row" and "+ Add a page" show the page types as tiles; the new page opens
  straight in its editor, named after its type. A page that still lacks what it needs is marked "unfinished", and
  Save waits until it is complete. Every page's editor ends with "Delete this page".
- **Thermostat on / off** (`switch`): a Switch item of its own as a pill under the ring, lit while on, e.g. a heat
  pump's on / off or a boost. Its label and icon can be set; with `"locked": true` off keeps the target (no minus
  and plus, the ring grey)
  ([docs/panel-config.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.12.0/docs/panel-config.md#thermostat)).
- **Keypad toggle buttons** (`"action": "toggle"`): a keypad button may switch an item of its own, without a code,
  lit while the item is on, e.g. a light beside the alarm's modes
  ([docs/panel-config.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.12.0/docs/panel-config.md#keypad)).
- **Thermostat and keypad pages in the swipe row**. A keypad's keys and buttons ignore a finger that swiped.
- **Shortcuts on home** may open their page while their item is off too (`open_when_off`, default off).
- **The demo** shows the NSPanel US too (switch in its note at the bottom).

**Changed**
- Discs and round corners are drawn round, row by row: the keypad's key flash, the thermostat's minus and plus,
  the media page's play button, a light's colour, the pills.
- The info lines on home: an icon on the left (and its badge) sits centred on its line instead of low and cut off;
  the two lines are 3 px further apart.
- The web interface: labels and titles may be as long as the configuration allows, with a note how the display
  shows one that is too wide for its place (a button's name has more room on the NSPanel US); optional items are
  marked as such on the media, weather and thermostat pages.

**Fixed**
- The keypad's icons sat low beside their text on the display.
- The web interface's preview: a badge's number sat high in its box.

**Install**
- An NSPanel EU: Settings > Firmware with `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.12.0.bin` from
  `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-0.12.0.zip`, then write the display design `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-303ff343.tft`.
  This once by hand: 0.11 has no update check yet; from 0.12 on, Check for updates does it.
- An NSPanel US: the same from `ohpanel-nspanel-us-0.12.0.zip`, the display design
  `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-ef953e12.tft`; a new one: `ohpanel-nspanel-us-factory-0.12.0.bin` over the serial
  port, flash erased first ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.12.0/docs/install.md)).
- Coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image over the serial port, flash erased first.
- Each zip holds that panel's factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates over WiFi and the display
  design, and the licences that go with the binaries.
