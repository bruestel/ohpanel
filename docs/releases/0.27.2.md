Camera pictures on an image page no longer restart the panel. Only the firmware: the display design is the one of
0.27.0.

**Fixed**
- **A camera's snapshot restarted the panel**: a snapshot is drawn as ten thousands of small rectangles, and making
  them took several large blocks of memory at once. When one of them could not be had, the panel restarted. They
  now take far less memory at a time; a picture the memory left cannot hold is not shown (the log says so), the
  panel keeps running.
- **A camera that takes its time**: a picture waits up to 15 seconds for its answer (was 5). A camera's bridge
  takes some seconds for a fresh snapshot, and over WiFi the panel gave up on it.
