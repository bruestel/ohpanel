Covers come in parts, so they no longer need a large block of memory, and the log says why a picture is not shown.
The display design is the one of 0.13.0: panels on 0.13 or later need only the firmware.

**Fixed**
- **Covers need no large block of memory**: a cover is now downloaded (or taken from an Image item) in parts of
  16 KB, and the JPEG decoder reads across them. Before, a cover had to fit in one piece: the panel's 2 MB PSRAM often
  has 1.4 MB free but its largest free block may be well under that (737 KB right after the start), so a cover of a
  few hundred KB could fail. 0.15.1 already kept that from restarting the panel; now such a cover is shown.

**Changed**
- **Why a cover is not shown**: the panel decodes baseline JPEGs only. Anything else is now recognised by its first
  bytes and its download stopped at once, with the reason in the log: `a PNG` (or a GIF, a WebP, a web page),
  `the panel decodes only JPEG`; `a progressive JPEG, the panel decodes only baseline ones`; `larger than 400 KB`.
  The icon stays in each case.
- **The largest free PSRAM block** in the log's health line, in Diagnostics and in the web interface's system
  information, next to the free PSRAM.

**Install**
- Panels on 0.13 or later: Settings > Firmware > Check for updates installs the firmware; the display design stays.
- Panels on 0.12 or older: the same, then the display design it offers.
- An NSPanel EU by hand: `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-firmware-0.15.2.bin` from `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-0.15.2.zip` (display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-4e7603bf.tft`, unchanged).
- An NSPanel US by hand: the same from `ohpanel-nspanel-us-0.15.2.zip` (upright, display design
  `ohpanel-nspanel-us-display-67c9b1ac.tft`) or `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-0.15.2.zip` (on its side, display
  design `ohpanel-nspanel-us-landscape-display-26f73ca8.tft`).
- A new panel, or coming from Tasmota or Sonoff's firmware: flash the factory image of its zip over the serial port,
  flash erased first ([docs/install.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.15.2/docs/install.md)).
- Each zip holds that panel's factory image (serial, offset 0), the firmware for updates over WiFi and the display
  design, and the licences that go with the binaries. `elf-ohpanel-0.15.2.zip`: the `.elf` files to decode a core
  dump ([docs/crash-analysis.md](https://github.com/bruestel/ohpanel/blob/v0.15.2/docs/crash-analysis.md)).
