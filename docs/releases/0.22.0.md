The panel's buzzer sounds: a tone for touches and for notifications, their volumes and a tone to play set from
openHAB. Only the firmware: the display design is the one of 0.21.0.

**New**
- **Notifications sound** as they show: by default `rising` for info, `falling` for a warning, `alarm` for an alert, at
  65 %. One that waits for a touch on a screen that is off (at night, idle at 0 %) is quiet; one that wakes the screen
  sounds. `"none"` silences a level (`display.sound.notifications`).
- **A tone for each touch** that does something (`display.sound.touch`, off by default): a soft `click`, a bright
  `tick` or any other tone, at 12 % unless set otherwise. Swipes and sliders are quiet.
- **Two volumes**, `touch_volume` and `notification_volume`, each kept in step with an openHAB item if you like (a
  Dimmer or Number), as the screen's brightness: to mute the panel at night, for example.
- **A tone from openHAB** (`display.sound.play`): a String item; `ding-dong` set on it plays at the notification volume,
  `ding-dong|30` at 30 %. The panel then sets it to NULL. The doorbell on the panel.
- **Nine tones**: `click`, `tick`, `beep`, `double`, `triple`, `rising`, `falling`, `ding-dong`, `alarm`.
- **In the web interface**: the Panel page's settings are tiles of their own (Display, Screen, Notifications, Sound,
  Pages and openHAB) in columns under the pages, and Advanced is always open. The Sound tile has ▶ beside each tone,
  which plays it on the panel before it is saved (`POST /api/v1/sound`).

Panels with notifications sound after the update; set `notification_volume` to 0, or a level to `"none"`, to keep
them quiet.
