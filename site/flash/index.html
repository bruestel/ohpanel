OHpanel flasher

The first installation on a Sonoff NSPanel, from the browser

Mains off. Take the panel off the wall and disconnect it from mains before you open it. The serial adapter powers it with 3.3 V: never connect both.

Before you click

  1. Open the panel and connect a 3.3 V USB serial adapter: GND–GND, ESP_RX–TX, ESP_TX–RX (RX, TX on the US version), 3V3–3.3 V (with photos).
  2. Tie IO0 to ground, then plug the adapter in: the panel waits in its bootloader.
  3. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (the browser needs Web Serial).

Install

This browser cannot talk to a serial port: use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or esptool. The flasher needs a secure page (https).

Tick Erase device when the panel ran other firmware before (Sonoff's, Tasmota, ESPHome). The factory image comes from the latest release, or the test build when you choose it: something to try, not yet tested as well.

Afterwards

  1. Take the wire off IO0, unplug the adapter and plug it in again: OHpanel starts.
  2. Close the panel and put it back on the wall.
  3. Join its open WiFi ohpanel-xxxxxx, choose your network, connect openHAB and write the display design (ohpanel-nspanel-eu-display-<version>.tft, about 15 minutes): step by step.
